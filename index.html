Common questions

Before you go live.

How does Camera Man connect to a director?

The director opens a session on Go, Tab or Studio. The operator scans the displayed QR code from the Camera Man side of LivCast Go, then sends footage over the same Wi‑Fi network or hotspot.

How many smartphones can I connect?

LivCast Go supports up to four Camera Man devices, LivCast Tab supports up to eight, and LivCast Studio supports up to 20, depending on the capabilities of your computer and network.

Can the director communicate with Camera Man?

Yes. The director can send a written instruction to one operator or broadcast it to the entire connected camera crew. Remote zoom and torch controls are also available for connected Camera Man devices.

Can I connect professional cameras?

LivCast Studio is designed to combine Camera Man smartphones with DSLR and cinema camera inputs. Go and Tab accept Camera Man devices, built-in cameras and video links.

Where can LivCast stream?

LivCast supports destinations such as Facebook and YouTube, including direct Facebook streaming, with additional platforms and custom stream workflows available according to the version.

Which religious texts are included?

The King James Version Bible is currently built into all three LivCast experiences. Quran support is planned for a future update.
